    Q&A

    Common questions

    How do I book?

    Use “Reserve now” above, pick a date and time in the calendar, then submit your details. Julia will confirm.

    Can I change or cancel?

    Contact the studio as soon as your plans shift. If you are signed in on this device, you can cancel from your booking list.

    Do I need a trial for bridal makeup?

    A trial is recommended so the look is locked in before the big day — but it is optional depending on your timeline.

    What if I have sensitive skin?

    Mention it in your notes when booking. Julia uses professional products and can adjust the routine to your needs.
