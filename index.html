Before you arrive

A few tips so your appointment goes smoothly

Skin

Come with a clean face

Arrive without makeup if you can, and skip heavy creams right before the session.

References

Bring inspiration

Save a few photos you like — it helps align on tone, intensity, and finish.

Outfit

Neckline & jewelry

Wear or bring something close to your event neckline so the look feels cohesive.

Timing

Plan a little buffer

Artistry takes time — allow a few extra minutes so we never have to rush the details.

Q&A

Common questions

How do I book?

Use the booking section above to pick an open slot, or reach out by email or phone. Julia will confirm the details.

Can I change or cancel?

Contact the studio as soon as your plans shift and we will adjust the appointment together.

Do I need a trial for bridal makeup?

A trial is recommended so the look is locked in before the big day — but it is optional depending on your timeline.

What if I have sensitive skin?

Mention it when you reach out. Julia uses professional products and can adjust the routine to your needs.
